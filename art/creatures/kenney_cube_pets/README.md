# Kenney — Cube Pets (2.0)

CC0 1.0 (see LICENSE.txt). By Kenney (www.kenney.nl). Added by the director on 2026-11-06.

**Use in Godot:** `GLB format/` (its `Textures/colormap.png` is the colour map all the models share).
24 animals: beaver, bee, bunny, cat, caterpillar, chick, cow, crab, deer, dog, elephant, fish, fox, giraffe, hog, koala, lion, monkey, panda, parrot, penguin, pig, polar bear, tiger.
Clips: idle, walk, run, eat, dance, gesture-positive, gesture-negative, static. Rigid parts (no skeleton). `Previews/` has an image of each.

`source/` (ignored by Godot through `.gdignore`): FBX and OBJ copies with their textures, Kenney's overview page and links.
