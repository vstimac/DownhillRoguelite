# Reno 4

The summit's old Renault 4 (environment/SummitRadio.cs). A Tripo model supplied by the director as
`reno4.zip` (2026-11-10): an FBX of 50k triangles with 4K PBR textures. Converted in Blender 5.1 to
`reno4.glb`: scaled to 3.7 m long (a real Renault 4), its length along Z, wheels on the origin's
ground, base colour cut to 2048 px and the other maps to 1024 px, all saved as JPEG (4.4 MB instead
of 63 MB). Godot extracts the textures next to the model. The original FBX is not in git.
