# Original procedural foliage prototypes

Three pine variants and three grass tufts, generated by `tools/forest/generate_forest.gd`.
No external tree asset, AI bitmap, texture atlas or third-party dependency is required for these
meshes. They are clearly labelled art prototypes, not scanned or final production trees.

Regenerate from the project root with Godot .NET:

```
godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/forest/generate_forest.gd
```

Pines have tapered trunks with a flared, rooted foot, drooping two-part branch whorls (darker and
older low down, fresh growth and a leader spike on top), irregular needle sprays shaded from base to
tip, a green of their own per variant (blue spruce, plain pine, sunny yellow-green) and baked index
LODs (9264 / 3648 / 2368 triangles). They use the wind material (terrain/foliage_tree.gdshader):
opaque vertex colours, no alpha cards, with each vertex's flex in UV.x (0 trunk, 1 twig tip) and a
per-branch phase in UV.y, so the trunk sways and the branches flutter.
Trunks are centred at the scene origin and 12 m reference height; runtime fitting sets actual
10–18 m mature heights or 2.5–5 m saplings and matching trunk collision radii. The symmetric custom bounds preserve trunk
alignment despite asymmetric crowns. Grass tufts have 10 / 13 / 16 curved, tapering blades
(50 / 65 / 80 triangles), dark at the root and light at the tip with a little yellow or blue per
blade, fitted to 0.12–0.4 m heights; the shared grass material (terrain/forest_grass.gdshader) bends
them in the wind's rolling gusts and parts them round the carrier. See debug/FoliageLab.tscn. The meshes are baked once; they are not regenerated during streaming.

Generation uses fixed seeds. Further tree species, saplings and a production foliage pass remain
art work, and must be reviewed from ground level as well as distant silhouettes.

## Broadleaf trees and snags (the deeper circles)

`broadleaf_{green,dark,fall}_{0,1,2}`: about 8 m tall, a rooted trunk splitting low into three or
four limbs that fork once, crowned by lumpy faceted leaf clusters (a once-split icosahedron each,
lit from above; the coarse LODs use the unsplit one), an inner ring and a cap so the crown reads as
one round canopy (1258–1524 / 418–504 / 338–408 triangles). Autumn trees mix red and yellow
clusters into the orange. `snag_{0,1}`: bare, grey, crooked dead trees (140–156 triangles, no LODs).
All carry the same wind data as the pines. TerrainSettings uses them from Lust down (green in Lust,
dark in Gluttony, autumn in Greed, snags with a few leafy trees in Wrath, Heresy and Violence; the
small pines in Fraud), and every circle's grass is the generated tufts.

## Bushes and dwarf pines (every circle's shrubs)

`bush_{green,dark,fall,dry}_{0,1}`: about 1.25 m mounds of the same lumpy leaf clusters as the
broadleaf crowns on a few short stems (664–752 triangles), in a summer green, a dark green, autumn
and a dry, dusty olive. `dwarf_pine_{0,1}`: knee-to-waist krummholz, a crooked low trunk under flat
needle sprays (380–456 triangles). Same wind material and data as the trees. They replace the
art-pack bushes, which were flat-coloured stars; TerrainSettings picks them per circle. Bushes
fitted taller than 1.2 m block the carrier (TerrainChunk's cover collision).

Regenerate only these (the existing trees and grass are skipped):

```
godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/forest/generate_forest.gd -- --only=bush,dwarf_pine
```
