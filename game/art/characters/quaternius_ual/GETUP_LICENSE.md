# GetUp_Back clip

The `GetUp_Back` animation in `UAL_Mannequin.glb` is the clip "Move_get_up_from_the_ground" from the
KinemIQ Free Animation Pack (CC0 1.0), `Kinemiq/free-game-animations` v1.0, file
`KinemIQ_Free_Animations_CC0_GLB.zip` > `GLB/Move_get_up_from_the_ground.glb`. Its motion comes from
the Universal Animation Library by Quaternius (CC0 1.0); KinemIQ retargeted and exported it. Licence
text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Retargeted onto this rig: bone names mapped from the UE-style names to the mannequin's `DEF-` bones
(deforming bones only), each rotation applied as a delta from the source's end (standing) pose onto
the mannequin's `Idle_Loop` pose, and the hips' travel carried over unscaled. Starts face up on the
floor and ends in `Idle_Loop`. Only the back variant exists; no CC0 face-down get-up was found.
