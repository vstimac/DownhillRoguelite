# audio

Sound effects, ambience and music.

- `music/descent_day.wav`, `music/descent_night.wav`: original ambient loops, generated by `tools/generate_ambient.py` (no samples or outside material). `MusicPlayer` crossfades them by daylight and ducks them under the narrator.
- `narrator/NARR_###.wav`: the 500 voiced narrator lines (ElevenLabs, generated from `narrative/data/metadata.csv`), played by `narrative/NarratorDirector`.
- `footsteps/<surface>_<n>.wav`: synthesized by `tools/generate_foley.py`; `Footsteps` plays a step per planted foot by surface (snow when it's snowing), recorded Kenney steps where there are some. (`generate_foley.py` and `generate_weather_sounds.py` also make a fire, wind and rain loop; the game now uses recorded ones from Sonniss instead, so those files aren't kept.)
- `sonniss/`: clips cut from the Sonniss #GameAudioGDC 2026 bundle by `tools/import_sonniss.py` (fixed cut times, mono one-shots or crossfaded loops, levelled by measured loudness, 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis): sword, shield and blows (`combat/`), the jade glutton and the beasts' growls, roars and shrieks, a web torn (`creatures/`), the station fire and the torch (`fire/`), wind, rain and thunder (`weather/`), the cart, the pack, clothes, mending, a page, a whoosh, a music box (`foley/`), a station bell, an achievement and the end of a run (`ui/`) and one ambience bed per circle (`ambience/`, played by `CircleAmbience`). Every clip's library, original file and cut is in `docs/audio/sonniss_sources.csv`; the whole bundle is indexed in `docs/audio/sonniss_gdc2026_index.csv` (`tools/index_sonniss.py`); which sound comes from where is in `docs/audio/coverage.md`. Sonniss only fills in where we have no recording of our own.
- Our own recordings (`original_recordings.csv` lists every clip: the recording it was cut from, its cut times, suggested use, level and, for voice lines, the words). Brought in by `tools/import_recordings.py <unpacked audio pack>`; they come before anything synthesized or from a sound library, which only fill in for what we can't record ourselves.
  - `creatures/mimic_<kind>_<n>.wav` (growls, hiss, snarl, snort, breath, attacks): the voice of the pack fiend, the backpack enemy (`wildlife/enemies/packbeast.tres`).
  - `creatures/cup_<kind>_<n>.wav` (the Cup of Pohlepe: laugh, short vocals, grunts, attack, exertion): the voice of the bog ogre (`wildlife/enemies/ogre.tres`).
  - `voice/npc_hz_putnik/hr-HR/HZ_###_<words>.wav`: 23 Croatian lines for HŽ, the eternal traveller, levelled to one loudness. TODO: the NPC itself is a placeholder; the lines aren't played yet.
  - `voice/npc_salterusa/hr-HR/SALTERUSA_<category>_###.wav`: 97 Croatian clips for the šalteruša, the counter clerk (`narrative/salterusa`), cut by the team at pauses from 14 recordings by category (barks, idle, quest, documents, gear, refusals, threats, death and return, rare and philosophical lines, breaths and sighs, "Bože dragi", "Piše vam lijepo na vratima", "Ja vama mogu objasniti"). Each was brought to the same speaking loudness, measured without the pauses (speech_db, -21 dB like the narrator); breaths and sighs sit at -27 dB. The words aren't written down yet (the `words` column is empty). A different character from HŽ: a woman's part in another recording, where HŽ is a man ("Već sam mislio...").
  - 14 of the creature clips clip (the recordings ran too hot); fine for monsters, but record the next ones with more headroom.
- Buses (`default_bus_layout.tres`): Music, Voice and Ambience, set from the pause menu.
- `creatures/<beast>_<kind>_<n>.wav`: the rest of the night beasts' voices (she-wolf growl, snarl and howl; leopard growl, snarl and chuff; lion growl, snarl and roar), synthesized by `tools/generate_beast_sounds.py`, and the giant spider hiss, shriek and chitter (plus `web_tear_<n>.wav`, a spider web torn), by `tools/generate_spider_sounds.py`; `NightBeast` growls before a charge, snarls as it charges or steals food, and calls now and then while it stalks.
