# wildlife

Animals on the slopes (M9), and the beasts of the night. The animals live like animals on a mountain (Valheim's wildlife is the reference for how they sense and react): they graze, wander, can be stalked, hunted and angered. The night beasts hunt (see `docs/night-beasts.md`).

- `WildlifeSpecies` (resource): model, scale and rotation (the packs differ), which clip is idle, graze, rest, walk, flee and death, speeds, senses (`Temperament`: skittish, wary, curious, bold, aggressive; `SightRange`, `ViewAngle`, `HearingRange`, `AlertSeconds`; flight and safe distances; curious range), group size and herd spacing, hunting (`Health`, `AttackDamage`, `AttackInterval`, `Loot`, `AlwaysLoot`), the chase of aggressive ones (`ChaseLeash`, `ChaseSeconds`, `ChaseRest`), and habitat: circles, surfaces, steepest slope.
- `default_wildlife.tres`: all species. Limbo has cows, horses, sheep, llamas, rats and frogs; Lust horses and zebras; Gluttony pigs, rats and frogs; Greed llamas and rats; Wrath frogs and snakes; Heresy spiders; Violence snakes and wasps; Fraud snakes and spiders; Treachery rats; beyond the ninth, Kenney's cube pets. Skittish: sheep, zebras, rats, frogs, deer, bunnies, chicks. Wary: horses, snakes, spiders, foxes. Curious: cows, llamas, penguins, cats, dogs. Bold (neutral until struck): pigs, polar bears. Aggressive: wasps. Predators: wolves (Heresy to Treachery).
- `WildlifeSpawner`: keeps up to 36 animals in a 30 to 140 m ring around the carrier (removed beyond 180 m), mostly ahead downhill, on ground found by a ray; a group of two or more is a herd (`WildlifeHerd`); removes animals far away, well behind or long dead. Each animal watches the nearest carrier standing (`CarrierSense`: where, how fast, sprinting, falling, load, crouching = the brake key, torch, daylight; a guest's speed from its streamed feet, its crouch and load (in 20 kg steps) from its pose flags). A fall (DownedChanged, a guest's fall) scatters everything within 20 m, a sword swing (a guest's too, sent to the host) within 12 m, a hard landing (from 6 m/s) startles animals around. Adds the stealth eye. Reads only GameEvents, RunState and WorldClock.
- `WildlifeAnimal`: daily life: grazes, walks to the next spot along the slope (followers to their slot round the leader, keeping spacing), stands and looks round, lies down (big grazers; the model sinks, as the packs have no lying clip), drinks where `WaterNear` finds water (no water in the world yet). Senses: sight in a cone (`ViewAngle`), blocked by terrain, rocks and trees, shorter at night without a torch, head down, or for a still or crouching carrier; hearing of noise from speed, load and crouching. Suspicion builds from what it senses: suspicious it freezes, head up, facing the carrier (Alert); alerted it acts on its temperament: skittish ones bolt with their herd, away and downhill or along the slope, zig-zagging, stop at their safe distance, look back and graze again (uneasy for a while); wary ones walk off and look back; curious ones come up to a still carrier, sniff and lose interest; bold ones hold their ground; aggressive ones attack a carrier near their nest spot (where they were placed; wasps: within 15 m, a sting every 2.5 s), give up after a short chase (8 s, or 15 m from the nest), fly back and leave carriers alone for 6 s. Struck, a bold animal fights (charges and shoves) with the bold ones of its herd and calms down later. Every step ahead is checked: no steep banks, drops or rocks (cornered, a big one shoves). Pushed for `PushPatience` (3 s) it turns round, winds up for `ShoveWindUp` (0.6 s) and shoves the carrier about 4 m into the air (`ShoveHeight`); animals shorter than `MinShoverHeight` never shove (wasps sting instead). Hunting: health from `Health` (or its size); killed it plays its death clip or keels over, drops its `Loot` and `AlwaysLoot` through `KillLoot` (a hide and raw meat from the big ones, silk from spiders; raw meat cooks into cooked meat at a station's fire) and lies there `DeadSeconds`. Co-op: the host thinks; guests see position, clip, model pose and down (INetMob) and read awareness from the clip (`Aware`).
- Predators (`Temperament.Predator`; all their behaviour numbers in `PredatorTuning.cs`, which also holds the one switch `Enabled`): wolves, in packs of 2 to 4, on rock, scree, dirt and mud in the lower circles (Heresy to Treachery), three times as likely at night, one pack at a time. They sense like every animal. Once alerted a pack stalks: it circles its prey on a ring (18 m by day, 14 m at night) that closes to 8 m, then one wolf at a time rushes in, crouches and growls for 0.7 s (it fixes its aim halfway through: step aside or dive to make it miss) and springs for a 10 hp bite, backs off and circles again (first bite after 10 to 15 s by day, 5 to 8 s at night; then every 6 to 9 s, or 3.5 to 6 s). Any sword hit or kick scares a wolf off for 45 s; half the pack hurt or dead, the whole pack retreats for 90 s. A fall, hard landing or swing near them draws them in instead of scaring them, and a downed carrier is bitten sooner; a carrier within 25 m of a station's fire (or in a shelter) is left alone (they keep 30 m off), one bleeding out in co-op is circled but never bitten. A killed wolf drops a hide and raw meat. Co-op: each wolf picks its prey among all carriers (`WildlifeSpawner.PickPrey`: nearest, a fallen one first, never one at a fire, sticking with its prey); guests see the crouch in the streamed model pose and read the wind-up from the clip (idle played fast), and growl with it. Each machine tells its own carrier once a run when wolves hunt it and when one crouches to spring.
- `StealthEye`: the eye above the hotbar: nearly shut (unaware), half open amber (suspicious), wide open red (alerted), for the strongest awareness within 35 m; shown while crouching or while an animal near is suspicious or alerted.

- Night beasts: `BeastSpecies` (resource: model, shade, glowing eyes, stalking, charge, throw, circles), `BeastRoster` (the list; `default_beasts.tres`: she-wolf, leopard, lion, and from the fourth circle the giant spider: the Quaternius enemy pack's Spider). `SpiderWebs` (added by BeastDirector) strings `SpiderWeb`s between close pairs of trees from the fourth circle on; walking into one snares the carrier (GameEvents.Snared); `debug/TestSpiderWebs.tscn` shows them, `NightBeast` (stalk, wind-up, charge, retreat, leave) and `BeastDirector` (brings them after dark, one attacker at a time). In `debug/TestTerrain.tscn` as the `Beasts` node.

Debug: `debug/TestWildlife.tscn` lines up every species beside a 1.8 m pole; `WildlifeDebugOverlay` (F3) shows count, cost and state. Tests: `debug/tests/WildlifeTests.tscn` (population, behaviour on a test field: alert, herd flight and calm-down, curious approach, wary and bold, stealth, hunting, fighting back, stings, the stealth eye, cliff edges, banks and rocks; wolves: stalking, the wind-up and dodging it, stealth, night, being scared off and the pack's retreat, falls, fires, co-op prey; also the shove; `-- --predators-only` runs just the wolves) and `debug/tests/BeastTests.tscn`. Night beasts at once: `godot --path . -- --hour=23 --beasts-now`.

`debug/tests/WildlifePhysicsTests.tscn` checks support removal, displacement, resting and capsule contact on a 45-degree slope. The population test measures the capsule's bottom sphere against its actual support plane; a vertical ray below its origin gives misleading gaps on slopes and obstacle edges.

Population tests print their RNG seed. To replay the spawn selection, append `-- --wildlife-seed=8` when running `WildlifeTests.tscn`. `WildlifeSpawner.PopulationSeed` can also be set before its first tick. Normal gameplay still picks a random population seed.
